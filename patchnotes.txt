# Version 1.9
## New
- Added cries for all the new Pokémon.
- All attacks introduced in the game now have their own animations.
- Updated the following Pokémon's abilities to match what they have in Pokémon Champions: Mega Absol Z, Mega Garchomp Z, Mega Lucario Z, Mega Golisopod, and Mega Baxcalibur.
- Combined the move deleter and move relearner in Pokémon Centers into a single NPC, and added a new NPC that can export your team to Pokémon Showdown format.
## Balancing
- Pixie Punch now never misses.
- Reduced Dark Wave PP from 15 to 10.
- Reduced Spectral Strike's power from 90 to 80.
- Reduced Pressure Wave's power from 80 to 75.
- Reduced Crystalline Cannon's accuracy from 100 to 90.
- Increased Heist's power from 40 to 55.
- Reduced Seismic Beam's power to 75, but gave it a 20% chance to lower the target's Special Defense by 1 stage.
- Added the following moves as TMs: Power Surge, Raging Blast, Pressure Wave, Spectral Strike, Haunt, Seismic Beam, Frostbite, Telekinetic Slam, Crystalline Cannon, and Metal Pulse.
- Added the following moves as move tutor moves: Dark Wave, Blitz, Thunder Slash, Fae Strike, Pixie Punch, Scorching Blade, Vengeance, and Mineral Burst.
- Added the new moves to the learnsets of all Pokémon, both old and new, where they fit.
## Other
- Reduced how often weather occurs on most maps.
## Bug Fixes
- Fixed Ewan not having teams if the player skips gym battles after fighting Ewan.
- Fixed a trainer appearing twice on Route 13.
- Fixed weather not instantly disappearing from the screen when indoors.
- Fixed the Sleeping Bag causing time to progress even if the player backed out of the Sleeping Bag menu.
- Fixed a random spot on the Town Map displaying Route 7 where it shouldn't.
- Fixed route music missing from Route 18.
- Fixed the Forgotten Temple name being visible on the map before reaching it.
- Fixed a sign in Penetun Gym displaying that Barboach can be caught instead of Tympole.
- Fixed interaction with Tia and Charles in Resec Town having repeated dialogue.
- Fixed the wrong dialogue box showing when interacting with Tia and Charles in Resec Town.
- Fixed Crystal repeating dialogue before her gym battle.
- Fixed Brawnsoon fainting if it had low HP when switching from Flood Form to Drought Form.
- Fixed the abilities Storm Surge and State Shift being able to be repressed or copied by other Pokémon.
- Qinlong, Yinlong, and Jinlong now have the correct abilities and hidden abilities.
- Fixed Haunt and Psy Barrage not displaying a message when the target became trapped.
- Fixed Gren-egg damaging allies and opposing Pokémon instead of only damaging all opposing Pokémon.
- Fixed Guiding Flame not damaging the target.
